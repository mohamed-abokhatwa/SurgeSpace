iiEvery element

Model it as built.

Network

  • Junctions, fixed- and varying-level sources, storage tanks
  • Pipes, including viscoelastic plastic pipes
  • Bends kept along the pipe and in the long section
  • Real map coordinates, background plans and aerials

Pumps and turbines

  • Pumps with inertia and four-quadrant characteristics
  • Variable-speed drives and motor starters (DOL, star–delta, soft start)
  • Hydro turbines with governors and runner characteristics
  • Pump curves fitted from points, data sheets or graphs

Valves

  • Line valves: gate, globe, ball, butterfly, needle or your curve
  • Non-return valves: ideal, delayed, or the dynamic characteristic
  • Pressure reducing, sustaining and breaking, flow control
  • Inline orifices

Air vessels

  • Vertical, horizontal or sphere; gas volume or bladder pre-charge
  • Dip tube, connection inertia, separate in and out losses
  • Check valve with bypass; polytropic gas
  • Sized automatically to a minimum-pressure target

Air valves

  • Double-acting, three-stage anti-slam, vacuum breaker, release only
  • Manufacturer curves; every orifice and Cd editable
  • Air-valve slam detected and shown on the map
  • Planned by AWWA M51 practice and tested in surge runs

Other protection

  • Surge tanks: simple, throttled, differential, variable area
  • One-way feed tanks
  • Surge anticipator and pressure relief valves
  • Timed blow-offs and burst discs
iiiTwo surge engines and daily operation

The right engine for each question.

Wave engine: method of characteristics on exact geometry, with column separation by the discrete gas cavity model and unsteady friction — for every fast event
Rigid-column engine for slow operations and surge-tank oscillation, on networks of thousands of pipes
Daily operation (extended period): demand patterns, controls and rules, leakage and pressure-driven demand, tank levels, energy
Water quality: water age, source tracing and chlorine
Steady state by the global gradient algorithm, as EPANET, with control valves

vDesign the protection

From the problem to the fix.

Sized for you

Automatic air vessel sizing, air valve planning and sizing, and a vessel sized with the air valve plan in place.

Least cost

A genetic algorithm chooses vessels, air valves, pipe classes or diameters for the lowest cost that passes the checks.

Checked

Design checks against pipe class, minimum pressure, column separation, device limits and turbine overspeed.

Uncertainty

Envelopes over wave speed, stroke time, inertia and time step — the range, not one guess.

Pipe forces

Unbalanced forces along each pipe for thrust blocks and supports.

Cases

Keep variants side by side, stored as differences; run them all and compare in one table.
