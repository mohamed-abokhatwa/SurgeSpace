SurgeSpace 3.0 — the biggest release yet.

Daily operation and water quality, a second surge engine, an AI copilot with your own AI account, live plant data, GIS and CAD links, and an Arabic interface.

Model from documents

  • Drawings, long sections, data sheets, spreadsheets and DXF read into items you check against the page
  • Plan drawings and Excel schedules become networks
  • Pump curves from data sheets or digitised by hand

Protection, designed

  • Air valve plan by AWWA M51 practice, tested in surge runs
  • Air vessel sized with the plan in place
  • Least-cost protection by genetic algorithm

AI copilot

  • Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Azure, Mistral, DeepSeek, xAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Together, or a free local model
  • Replies stream as they are written
  • Every change an undo step

Daily operation

  • Tanks, patterns, controls and rules
  • Leakage and pressure-driven demand
  • Water age, source tracing and chlorine — checked against EPANET 2.2

Engines and devices

  • Rigid-column engine for thousands of pipes
  • Four-quadrant pumps, variable-speed drives, motor starters
  • Differential surge tanks, turbine governors, viscoelastic pipes

Workspace

  • Start page, tour, command palette, staged run screen
  • Waterfall view and video export
  • Arabic interface and report; live data; GIS and CAD plug-ins
