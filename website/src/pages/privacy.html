Privacy policy · last updated 12 November 2026

Your models stay on your computer.

SurgeSpace is an engineering application for pressure-surge (water hammer) analysis. We do not sell or share personal data, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

What stays on your computer

Network models, project files, imported spreadsheets, drawings and documents, and all calculation results are processed and stored only on your computer. We never receive them.

What the app sends to our licence server

  • Licence activation and the daily check-in: your licence code, the computer's Machine ID (a code derived from the operating system's machine identifier; it does not contain your name), the computer name, the operating system and the SurgeSpace version. This is used only to manage your licence — which computers use it, and until when.
  • Evaluation: a one-way code of the computer (not reversible to the Machine ID or name), the operating system and the app version, to know when the 14-day evaluation started.
  • Only when you choose to send them: licence requests (name, company, email, message) and messages from Help → Questions and ideas (your text, an optional reply email, the app version and the screen you were on).

The optional AI features

The AI copilot and the model from documents use an AI account that you connect, with your own key. Your key is encrypted and stored on your computer. When you use an AI feature, the request — your question, the parts of the model it needs, or the pages of the documents you chose to read — goes directly from your computer to the provider you chose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), or to a model running on your own computer. It does not pass through our servers, and we never see your key, your questions or your documents. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send it. Nothing is sent until you ask.

Where it is stored

Licence data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers and D1), used only by Engineering Workspace. Messages may also be forwarded to our support inbox by email.

How long

Licence records are kept while the licence exists and for accounting afterwards. Evaluation records and messages are deleted on request.

This website

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or tracking. It is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which keep standard server logs to run the service. Links to WhatsApp, the Microsoft Store and email open those services, under their own terms.

Your choices

You can use SurgeSpace on the Free plan without activating anything; then nothing is sent except when you start an evaluation or send a message. To see or delete the data we hold about you, email support@engspace.app.
