Common questions

Before you write.

How do I get a licence?

In the app: Tools → Licence → Request a licence (personal for one computer, or company for several). Your Machine ID is included. Or email support@engspace.app.

I have a new computer.

Ask us to free the licence from the old computer, then enter your licence code on the new one.

Does SurgeSpace work offline?

Yes. A licensed computer checks in once a day when online and keeps working up to 60 days offline. The Free plan never needs a connection.

Which files can I import?

EPANET .inp, Excel and CSV tables, long-section profiles, DXF drawings with coordinates and levels, GeoJSON — and, with an AI account, PDF and image drawings and data sheets.

My model will not run on the Free plan.

The Free plan runs your own models up to 25 pipes (the built-in examples run at any size). Start the 14-day evaluation from the plan badge next to the SurgeSpace name, or activate a licence.

The AI does not answer.

Check the account under AI → AI accounts: the key, the model name, and — for Ollama or LM Studio — that the local server is running with cross-origin requests allowed. Errors from the provider are shown as they come.

Where is the manual?

Help → Quick start and the tour in the app; the full manual is in Docs on this site.
