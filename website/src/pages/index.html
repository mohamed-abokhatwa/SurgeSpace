iiiEverything a surge study needs

Every element, both engines, the protection.

Events

  • Pump trip with inertia run-down, controlled stop, start-up
  • Valve closure by stroke and law, or an operating curve
  • Turbine load rejection with governor and overspeed

Protection

  • Air vessels — bladder, dip tube, sized automatically
  • Air valves: double-acting, three-stage anti-slam, vacuum breaker
  • Surge tanks, feed tanks, anticipator and relief valves, burst discs

Two engines

  • Wave: method of characteristics with column separation
  • Rigid column for slow operations and large networks
  • Daily operation and water quality, as EPANET

Results

  • Long sections with envelopes, linked to the drawing
  • Waterfall view, graphs of any variable, slams on the map
  • A complete report with every assumption

Design

  • Least-cost protection by genetic algorithm
  • Checks against pipe class, minimum pressure, cavities
  • Uncertainty envelopes and pipe forces for thrust blocks

Your data

  • EPANET .inp, Excel and CSV, DXF and the AutoCAD plug-in
  • GIS: GeoJSON, QGIS and ArcGIS Pro plug-ins, maps with terrain
  • Live data over MQTT, OPC UA and Modbus

Questions

Asked often.

Is SurgeSpace free?

It is free to download. The Free plan runs all the built-in examples and your own models up to 25 pipes. A 14-day evaluation unlocks everything; after that, a personal or company licence. See Plans.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit, from the Microsoft Store. A Mac version is on the way.

Do I need an AI account?

No. Everything except the AI features works without one, including reading Excel schedules, EPANET files and DXF plans. If you want the copilot and the reading of drawings, connect your own account with any of 13 providers, or a free model running on your computer.

Can I bring my EPANET models?

Yes — .inp files import with their pumps, valves (including PRV, PSV and FCV), controls, patterns and vertices, and export back again.

How do I know the results are right?

Every engine is checked against a reference — EPANET 2.2, published transient benchmarks, closed-form theory and independent ODE solutions — and the checks run on every change. The full list is on Proven, with the methods in Theory and validation.

Who makes SurgeSpace?

Engineering Workspace, the engineering software house behind engspace.app, in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
